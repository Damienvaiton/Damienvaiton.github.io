Liner notes

The project

Built for the advanced programming course at the IUT in Bourg-en-Bresse, FastTrack is for F1 fans who want to follow the season without hunting for information everywhere.

The app fetches race data from an API, caches it locally and displays it in an interface designed to be read at a glance.

Tech stack

  • Kotlin
  • MVVM
  • Retrofit
  • Room
  • LiveData
  • Coroutines
What it does

The features

  1. Next race

    The next event on the calendar, with a countdown to the start.

  2. Full calendar

    Every date and every circuit of the season at a glance.

  3. Driver standings

    Each driver's points and position in the championship.

  4. Constructor standings

    The team hierarchy throughout the season.
